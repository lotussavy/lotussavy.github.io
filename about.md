<section class="about-section">
  <h2>Incoming Postdoctoral Appointment</h2>
  <p>
    I am an <strong>Incoming Postdoctoral Research Associate in Mechanical Engineering at <a href="https://www.baylor.edu/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Baylor University</a></strong>, starting August 17, 2026,
    working with <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=zvMPg9gAAAAJ&amp;hl=en&amp;oi=ao" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Dr. Liang Sun</a> in the <a href="https://avia.research.baylor.edu/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">AVIA (Advanced Vehicle Intelligence and Autonomy) Lab</a>. This
    appointment follows the completion of my Ph.D. in Information Systems at <a href="https://umbc.edu/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">UMBC</a>.
  </p>
  <p>
    The <a href="https://avia.research.baylor.edu/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">AVIA Lab</a> works at the intersection of aerospace and artificial intelligence, with research in
    machine learning, optimization, control, estimation, multi-agent coordination, energy modeling,
    uncertainty-aware decision making, advanced air mobility, drone systems, and human–AI interaction.
    My responsibilities will include designing and executing experiments, analyzing and validating data,
    developing research datasets and software, preparing scholarly and technical publications, supporting
    proposal development, mentoring students, and contributing to laboratory research infrastructure.
  </p>
</section>

<section class="about-section">
  <h2>Research Identity</h2>
  <p>
    My work sits at the intersection of Advanced Air Mobility demand modeling, neurosymbolic AI,
    forecasting, and optimization. I am interested in AI systems that are not only accurate, but also
    transparent enough for planners, researchers, and decision-makers to understand and use.
  </p>
  <p>
    Recent projects include demand forecasting for regional and urban air mobility, interpretable
    travel demand prediction using symbolic rules and neural models, and neural-accelerated
    optimization for pre-disaster mobility planning.
  </p>
</section>

<section class="about-section">
  <h2>What Drives My Work</h2>
  <p>
    Transportation systems are becoming more data-rich, automated, and complex. My research asks how
    machine learning, symbolic reasoning, and optimization can support safer planning decisions in
    settings where uncertainty, infrastructure constraints, policy choices, and human trust all matter.
  </p>
</section>
