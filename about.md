      <h1>About Kamal Acharya</h1>
      <p>
        I am a Postdoctoral Research Associate in Mechanical Engineering at
        <a href="https://avia.research.baylor.edu/person/kamal-acharya" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Baylor University</a>
        and a member of the
        <a href="https://avia.research.baylor.edu/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Advanced Vehicle Intelligence and Autonomy (AVIA) Lab</a>.
        My current work connects Advanced Air Mobility demand forecasts with aircraft assignment and scheduling,
        distributed optimization, and simulation to study energy-aware fleet operations and infrastructure planning.
      </p>
      <p>
        My doctoral research at UMBC focused on data-driven and neurosymbolic approaches to AAM demand modeling.
        Moving from demand prediction toward the operational decisions those forecasts inform continues to shape my work.
      </p>
      <p>
        Alongside research, I bring more than a decade of university teaching experience and have mentored students
        in research and engineering projects.
      </p>
