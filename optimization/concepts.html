Sources and scope

These are original concise teaching definitions and examples, reviewed with the foundation lessons and the primary references below. Variable types and heuristics provide vocabulary for later study; they do not imply that later lessons or solvers have been released. Bound directions are explicit, and a positive bound gap is not presented as proof of suboptimality.

  • Boyd, S., & Vandenberghe, L. (2004). Convex Optimization, Chapters 2 and 4: sets, feasible and optimal points, local optimality and attainment.
  • Google. OR-Tools: optimization problem components: objectives, constraints and feasibility.
  • Gurobi Optimization. Variables and constraints: domains, integrality and numerical feasibility.
  • Gurobi Optimization. Model attributes: incumbent values, objective bounds and solver-specific gap conventions.
