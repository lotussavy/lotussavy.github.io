Three ways to learn travel demand

04 · Experimental evidence

What improved, and what remains uncertain?

MAE ↓

Figure 3 shows lower error for combined input features than the original-only neural baseline across tested tree depths.

R² ↑

Combining original and rule-derived features generally increases explained variance. Performance does not rise monotonically with depth.

CPC ↑

Combined features yield better overlap between modeled and observed commuter flows in the reported comparison.

The paper compares original features, rules alone, and combined features using MAE, R², and Common Part of Commuters (CPC). Figures 3–5 contain the performance curves; this page does not reproduce unverified numerical points from the figure images.
