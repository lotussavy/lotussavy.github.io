A Roadmap for Forecasting AAM Demand

Before aircraft or vertiports can be deployed, planners must estimate who might travel, where journeys begin and end, which mode people would choose, and what the operational network can actually accommodate. Econometric methods

Use socioeconomic, geographic, and travel variables to estimate aggregate demand and interpret associations.

Why useful

Familiar statistical assumptions, interpretable coefficients and scenario analysis.

Modeling caution

Relationships estimated in existing travel markets may not transfer directly to a new AAM service.

03 · Research agenda

What is missing from today's AAM demand models?

The paper identifies coupled challenges spanning physical infrastructure, operations, traveler behavior and fair access. Select a theme, then a research gap, to explore the planning consequence.

Vertiport placement

A demand hotspot is not automatically a feasible vertiport location. Land availability, traveler access, charging facilities and local constraints affect the usable network.

Modeling direction: Joint demand-and-facility planning with access and site constraints
