Direct distillation

Prompt a teacher to generate explicit facts, rules or structured examples, then parse and review them.

Potential advantage

A relatively direct route to reusable knowledge artifacts.

Important limitation

Teacher hallucination and prompt sensitivity require verification.

No methodology is universally superior. These are qualitative interpretations, not measured model-performance comparisons.
