01 · Neuro-symbolic decision stack

From uncertain perception to checked decisions

The review discusses pipelines where a neural module detects concepts, a symbolic layer reasons about explicit domain knowledge, and a verifier checks constraints. Select each stage for its role and limitations.

→→
Neural concept extraction

A learned model converts raw observations into probabilistic concepts. Errors or shifts at this stage can mislead later reasoning.

Question to ask: Are detected concepts well grounded and reliable on unfamiliar inputs?

Forward reasoning: Observation → concepts → rules → checked decisionFeedback: violations or expert corrections can revise concepts and rules

A rule-checking step can detect certain inconsistencies, but it cannot guarantee correctness if the underlying concepts or specifications are wrong.

02 · Three complementary pillars

Trustworthiness is not a single score

Choose each dimension to see its goal, a common evaluation question and how symbolic knowledge may contribute. The comparisons are qualitative syntheses of the review, not a new quantitative experiment.

Robustness

Study whether a system preserves acceptable behavior when inputs are perturbed or the environment changes.

Evaluation question

Does the decision respect important constraints under noise or distribution shift?

Role of symbols

Explicit priors and logical verifiers can flag inconsistent predictions and support systematic generalization.

Two distinct sources of uncertainty
Aleatoric

Uncertainty inherent in noisy or ambiguous observations; additional training data cannot always eliminate it.

Epistemic

Uncertainty from incomplete model knowledge; may diminish with relevant evidence or improved modeling.

Confidence alone is not calibration. Probabilistic outputs, coverage and constraint consistency all require appropriate evaluation.

03 · Controlled intervention

Correct a concept, change a decision

A concept bottleneck makes an intermediate variable editable by a domain expert. The demonstration shows a hypothetical rule-based gate: if a hazard is detected, the system requests review instead of proceeding.
