My research · Advanced Air Mobility and AI

Understanding travel. Building better decisions.

I study where advanced air mobility could make a difference, how we can predict travel demand, and how neurosymbolic AI can make those predictions easier to understand. I also explore how forecasting can inform planning and optimization.

At Baylor University's AVIA Lab, I am building on this work by studying eVTOL scheduling, energy use, and fleet operations as part of a NASA University Leadership Initiative project.
