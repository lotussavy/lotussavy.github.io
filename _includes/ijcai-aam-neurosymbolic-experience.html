03 · Interpretable constraint example

When a proposed AAM dispatch encounters a rule

Change the hypothetical battery reserve and weather status, then enable or disable the symbolic gate. The example only explains decision logic: it does not calculate aircraft performance or establish operational approval.

Hypothetical ruleReserve ≥ 20 kWh AND no adverse-weather flagProposed decisionEligible for further review

The illustrative reserve exceeds the threshold and the adverse-weather flag is absent.

The 20 kWh threshold is invented for teaching and is NOT an aviation standard, certified minimum, empirical result or aircraft-specific operating limit. Passing the toy gate never means a real flight is safe.
