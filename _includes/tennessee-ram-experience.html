02 · Compare generalized travel costs

Cost + time + risk: what changes the choice?

Generalized Travel Cost (GTC) converts direct spending, travel time, and risk into comparable money-equivalent components. This illustrative scenario calculator shows the mechanics of an access–flight–egress journey, not a re-execution of the paper's calibrated regressions.

Illustrative positive cost magnitude
GTC = monetary expense + (hourly time value × hours traveled) + monetized risk

03 · Interpret the paper's results

When does RAM become more attractive?

The published scenario analysis highlights a combination of longer distances, substantial air-travel share, and higher ground generalized cost. Select each criterion to understand its role.

Travel distance

In the expanded-airport scenario, RAM becomes comparatively attractive for longer OD pairs, particularly beyond roughly 300 miles. This is a model-specific pattern, not a universal threshold.
